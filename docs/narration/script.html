Do not say clip numbers, filenames, keys, notes or destinations. Leave about 3 seconds of silence between clips; keep pauses within a clip comfortably under 2 seconds. Read numerals naturally as numbers. Keep the original master unchanged.

116 clips · 914 spoken words · 6.5–8.3 minutes of speech, or 12–14 minutes including the gaps.

No outstanding squirrel/alligator wording or role inconsistencies. Both experiments use Nora as actor and Eric as victim; the alligator escape wording is shared. Technical notes are expandable below each spoken paragraph.
